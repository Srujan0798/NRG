# /competitive-brief

Create a competitive analysis brief for one or more competitors or a feature area.

## Usage

```
/competitive-brief <competitor or feature area>
```

## When to Use

- Informing product strategy or feature prioritization
- Building sales battle cards
- Prepping board or investor materials
- Deciding where to differentiate vs. achieve parity

## Workflow

1. **Identify competitors** — List direct and indirect competitors in the space
2. **Gather intel** — Collect publicly available information (pricing, features, positioning)
3. **Compare dimensions** — Feature set, pricing, target audience, strengths, weaknesses
4. **Identify gaps** — Where competitors are weak and NRG can differentiate
5. **Recommend strategy** — Differentiate, parity, or ignore

## Output Format

```markdown
# Competitive Brief: [Feature/Area]

## Competitors Analyzed
| Competitor | Type | Key Offering |
|------------|------|-------------|
| [Name] | Direct/Indirect | [One-liner] |

## Feature Comparison
| Feature | NRG | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|---------|-----|-------------|-------------|
| [Feature] | ✅/❌ | ✅/❌ | ✅/❌ |

## Pricing Comparison
| Competitor | Model | Price Point |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| [Name] | [Model] | [Price] |

## Strengths & Weaknesses
### Competitor A
- **Strengths**: ...
- **Weaknesses**: ...

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **Differentiate**: ...
2. **Achieve Parity**: ...
3. **Ignore**: ...
```
