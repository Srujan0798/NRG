# /vendor-review

Evaluate a vendor with cost analysis, risk assessment, and recommendation.

## Usage

```
/vendor-review $ARGUMENTS
```

## Workflow

### 1. Define Requirements

- What capability is needed
- Must-have vs nice-to-have criteria
- Budget constraints
- Timeline

### 2. Vendor Profile

- Company background, size, stability
- Relevant experience and references
- Support and SLA terms
- Security and compliance posture

### 3. Cost Analysis

- Pricing model (subscription, usage-based, perpetual)
- TCO over 1, 3, and 5 years
- Hidden costs: implementation, training, integrations, support tiers
- Negotiation levers

### 4. Risk Assessment

- Vendor stability and concentration risk
- Data residency and sovereignty
- Exit strategy and data portability
- Integration complexity

### 5. Comparison and Recommendation

- Side-by-side scorecard
- Weighted scoring against requirements
- Clear recommendation with rationale
- Negotiation priorities

## Output

Vendor evaluation report with TCO breakdown, risk assessment, scorecard, and recommendation.
