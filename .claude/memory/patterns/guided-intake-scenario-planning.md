---
name: Guided Intake Scenario Planning
description: Recommendation and planning answers need structured intake, explicit assumptions, evidence-backed scenarios, gaps, risks, limitations, and a next validation action
type: feedback
---

External planning skills often combine useful structure with product-specific
presentation. For NRG, keep the structure and discard the career roleplay,
decorative report, and future-letter workflow unless a separate product surface
explicitly requires it.

**Rule:**

1. Do not jump from a broad planning question to advice. First identify the
   user's goal, known constraints, missing critical constraints, and assumptions.
2. If a path forward is requested, compare up to three bounded options:
   conservative, growth, and exploratory.
3. Each option must include evidence basis, expected outcome range, first
   milestone, capability/data/resource gaps, key risks, tier-safe limitations,
   and next validation action.
4. Scenario text is allowed to make trade-offs concrete, but it must be labelled
   as a scenario, not a prediction.
5. Do not roleplay as the user, claim to be a future version of the user, or
   convert advice into motivational fiction in the NRG core flow.
6. If recommendations depend on current markets, policy, salary, regulation, or
   public data, verify freshness and cite sources.
7. HTML or immersive reports must follow NRG frontend rules and cannot override
   the product's interface style.

**Why:** The useful pattern is guided intake plus option analysis. The unsafe
part is presenting uncertain future outcomes as emotionally immersive certainty.
NRG must stay evidence-first and audit-safe.

**How to apply:**

- Main answer flow should require planning recommendations to show intake,
  assumptions, scenarios, gaps, risks, limitations, and next validation.
- Acceptance gates should reject recommendation answers that lack the evidence
  basis or treat scenarios as guaranteed outcomes.
- Roleplay, future-self dialogue, and letter generation belong outside the core
  answer engine unless explicitly designed and reviewed as a separate feature.

**Source:** MiniMax `future-mirror` reviewed 2026-04-29; merged as guided
intake and scenario planning discipline, not as a career-planning product.
