A privacy-preserving research intelligence dashboard that provides **state-level and ministry-level aggregated data** on publications, patents, PhD output, funding, and startup ecosystems across institutions — with **zero individual-level records** and **no PII whatsoever**.

**Key compliance features to verify:**
- Data is aggregated to state/ministry level — no individual researcher or institute identifiable
- Financial figures rounded to nearest lakh — no precise amounts
- Temporal RBAC enforced — pre-publication data hidden until public release
- Full audit chain — every query logged with cryptographic attestation

**Session details:**
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Format:** Live system walkthrough + 10 representative queries + Q&A
- **Access:** Browser-based, no installation required (staging credentials provided)
- **No preparation needed**

2. Confirmation of the ministry department you represent
3. Any specific national-level aggregates or comparisons you'd like the system to demonstrate

**Significance:**
Your UAT sign-off constitutes Tier 2 acceptance under Protocol #45. Results will be formally documented and GPG-signed as part of the audit evidence package.

Please share your availability or reach out with any questions.
