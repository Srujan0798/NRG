# IIT/NIT Expansion Proposal
## National Research Intelligence Platform - 5-Institution Rollout Plan

---

## Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a comprehensive 18-month plan to expand the National Research Intelligence Platform (NRIP) to 5 premier Indian institutions, establishing a national research discovery network that will transform India's academic collaboration landscape.

---

## Expansion Strategy

### Phase 1: Pilot Institution (Months 1-6)
**Institution**: IIT Gandhinagar (Lead Institution)
- Already has technical expertise
- Serves as proof of concept
- Full platform deployment

### Phase 2: Initial Expansion (Months 7-12)
**Institutions**: IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi
- High-volume research data
- Established IT infrastructure
- Strong institutional support

### Phase 3: Scale Expansion (Months 13-18)
**Institutions**: IIT Madras, IISc Bangalore
- Top research output
- Diverse research domains
- Industry partnership opportunities
