## Executive Read

Finishing engineering produces the product. It does not, by itself, produce INR 50 lakh. The funding path is gated by commercial packaging, entity readiness, IP clarity, external assurance, first reference deployment, pricing, use-of-funds, and warm introductions.

The strongest first path is IITGN-routed seed or spin-off support, followed by parallel government grant and industry proof-of-contract paths. The commercial work must begin before the engineering closure wave finishes, because entity, IP, and third-party assurance timelines are longer than code timelines.

## Four Revenue Paths

| Path | What It Is | Priority | Expected Timeline | Main Friction |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| P1 | IITGN-routed seed, incubator, or spin-off support | 1 | 6-10 weeks | IP assignment and institutional approval |
| P2 | MeitY/DST research grant route | 2 | 4-6 months | Professor PI, proposal structure, milestone locks |
| P3 | Industry proof-of-contract route | 3 | 8-14 weeks | Warm introduction, entity paperwork, contracting |
| P4 | Licensing to research or academic data vendors | 4 | 4-6 months | IP clarity, valuation, exclusivity pressure |

Default execution stance: start P1 immediately, prepare P2 in parallel, and begin P3 warm-introduction work once C1/C2 are moving.

## Assignment Protocols

### C1 - Entity And Banking

**Owner:** founder + CA
**Done when:** entity is registered, GST/PAN are available, and a current account can receive institutional payments.
**Evidence:** certificate, GST/PAN proof, bank proof, CA note.

### C2 - IP Rights Letter

**Owner:** founder + IP counsel + IITGN IRPC
**Done when:** written terms confirm founder commercial rights or formal spin-off terms, with institute license terms stated clearly.
**Evidence:** signed letter or approved spin-off agreement draft.
**Risk:** this is the single largest commercial blocker.

### C3 - External Assurance

**Owner:** founder + security auditor
**Done when:** an external security and DPDP assurance engagement is signed, run, and closed with a letter.
**Evidence:** statement of work, findings register, final assurance letter.

### C4 - Reference Deployment

**Owner:** founder + product
**Done when:** IITGN or an equivalent first institutional user confirms a reference deployment and user-acceptance transcript.
**Evidence:** signed reference note, transcript, deployment URL or environment identifier.

### C5 - Buyer Narrative Deck

**Owner:** founder + writer
**Done when:** an eight-slide buyer narrative exists with problem, product, sovereign moat, traction, ask, milestones, team, and use-of-funds.
**Evidence:** PDF and source file.

### C6 - Pricing Model

**Owner:** founder + finance
**Done when:** three packages are priced with scope, seats, data volume, support, audit, and deployment assumptions.
**Evidence:** pricing memo and one-page quote template.

### C7 - Cap Table And Use-Of-Funds

**Owner:** founder + CA
**Done when:** INR 50 lakh is allocated across 12 months of salary, infrastructure, external assurance, commercialization, legal, and contingency.
**Evidence:** spreadsheet and CA-reviewed summary.

### C8 - Warm Introduction Tracker

**Owner:** founder + advisors
**Done when:** each path P1/P2/P3 has at least one named warm route, introducer, ask, next step, and target date.
**Evidence:** tracker with status and latest action.

## Commercial Readiness Gate

NRG is INR 50 lakh-ready only when:

- C1 legal entity and banking are complete.
- C2 IP rights are in writing.
- C5 buyer narrative deck exists.
- C6 pricing model exists.
- C7 use-of-funds model exists.
- C8 has at least three warm routes.

C3 and C4 materially improve close probability and should be started immediately, even if they close after the first conversations begin.

## Caveat

Entity structure, tax treatment, grant eligibility, and IP terms require verification by a Chartered Accountant and IP/technology counsel before signing or quoting anything.
