Subject: National Research Graph spin-off and IP rights clarification

We request written confirmation of the IP and commercialization position for National Research Graph (NRG), including:

1. Ownership of code, architecture, documentation, deployment artifacts, and generated evidence.
2. Rights for the founder to commercialize NRG as a spin-off, license, or service.

4. Treatment of IITGN-provided, ministry-provided, and third-party data.
5. Restrictions on external deployments, publications, marketing, and attribution.
6. Approval path for the first external reference customer.
7. Any revenue share, equity, licensing, or reporting terms.

Requested evidence: signed letter, approved spin-off agreement draft, or email confirmation from the authorized IITGN office.
