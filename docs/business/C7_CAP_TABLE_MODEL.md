# C7 Cap Table And Use Of Funds Model

**Evidence file:** `docs/business/C7_CAP_TABLE.xlsx`  
**Status:** Generated model, founder/CA review required.

## Model Tabs

- `Cap Table`: founder ownership, INR 50L and INR 1Cr raise scenarios, dilution, post-money.
- `Use Of Funds`: 12-month allocation across engineering, infra, audit, data, commercialization, legal, and contingency.
- `Burn`: monthly burn and runway estimates.

## Assumptions

- Scenario A: INR 50L raise at INR 4Cr pre-money.
- Scenario B: INR 1Cr raise at INR 8Cr pre-money.
- Founder owns 100% pre-financing unless updated by counsel.
- Amounts are planning estimates, not CA-reviewed financial statements.
