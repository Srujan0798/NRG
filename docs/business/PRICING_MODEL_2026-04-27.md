# NRG Pricing Model

**Status:** Founder draft. Validate with CA, legal counsel, and first customer procurement constraints.

| Package | Target Buyer | One-Time Setup | Annual Recurring | Included Scope |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Pilot | University department or incubator | INR 8L | INR 6L/year | single institute, up to 5M rows, 25 named users, quarterly security review |
| Sovereign Campus | IIT/NIT/private university | INR 18L | INR 14L/year | 3-node deployment, 50M rows, 100 users, UAT, drift monitoring, DR drill |
| Ministry Network | Ministry or multi-institute program | INR 40L+ | INR 30L+/year | multi-tenant deployment, 600GB ingest, external audit support, SLA, custom reports |

## Quote Assumptions

- Buyer provides clean data license and source-system access.
- External CERT-In or DPDP audit fees are pass-through unless bundled separately.
- Travel, physical server procurement, and cloud credits are billed separately.
- Any PII-bearing dataset requires a signed data-processing agreement before ingestion.
