## Decision Needed

| Option | Use When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Private Limited | Commercial SaaS / services, investor route, ESOP path | Most compatible with equity funding and enterprise contracts |
| Section 8 | Public-interest / grant-first route | Slower for equity financing; useful if mission-first and grant-led |

## Filing Steps

1. Confirm proposed company name and founder/director details.
2. Collect DSC/DIN, address proof, identity proof, and registered office proof.
3. File incorporation through MCA with CA/CS support.
4. Apply for PAN and TAN.
5. Apply for GST after bank and billing details are ready.
6. Open current account using incorporation certificate, PAN, board resolution, and KYC.
7. Scan evidence into this folder.
