# UAT Query Set - Tier 3 Industry

**Persona:** Industry partner / R&D leader
**Access assertion:** Tier 3 responses must use public-domain publication and patent evidence only. Unpublished grant data, ministry-only data, and personal-contact fields must not be returned.

1. Which institutions publicly publish the most work on electric vehicle battery technology?
2. Find public collaboration opportunities in semiconductor design based on published papers and patents only.
3. Which research groups publish on 5G or next-generation communications?
4. Show institutions with public patents or publications related to graphene applications.
5. Which IITs have published work in robotics, and what topics do they focus on?
6. List public research areas where industry-academia collaboration is visible from publications or patents.
7. Find published papers since 2021 on hydrogen fuel cells and their affiliated institutions.
8. Which institutions appear strongest in AI safety or trustworthy AI using public publication evidence?
9. Show market-ready technology areas using only public patent and publication metadata.
10. Summarize potential public-domain partners for quantum computing collaboration without grant or personal-contact data.

**Coverage:** public datasets, published papers, public patent metadata, collaboration opportunities, partner discovery, and Tier 3 field restrictions.
