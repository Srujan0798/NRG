==================

  - 100 users all requesting synthesis simultaneously
  - Anthropic API rate limits → requests queue
  - JWT signing (RS256) also adds ~3s under load

KEY INSIGHT
===========

  Under concurrent load, LLM synthesis becomes the bottleneck.
  Recommendation: Pre-warm ALL critical queries in cache before load test.
