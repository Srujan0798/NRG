BOTTLENECK ANALYSIS
===================
  K-4 single-query fast path is fixed: the same publication-count query completes in 347.114ms cold and 96.003ms warm.
  K-2 still fails under 100-user concurrency because the local API queues heavily under load.

  Evidence:
    - /query minimum response was 45.412ms, proving the fast path can execute quickly.
    - /query median rose to 3.9s and P99 rose to 38s under concurrency.
    - /auth/login P99 was 46s, so login/JWT/auth middleware also saturates the local process during ramp-up.
    - Rate limiting and prompt-sanitiser cross-user poisoning were reduced by TRUST_PROXY_HEADERS=1 and NRG_QUOTA_DISABLED=1.
    - Per-request INFO logging was reduced with LOG_LEVEL=ERROR, so stdout flooding is not the remaining primary cause.
