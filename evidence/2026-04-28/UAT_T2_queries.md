# UAT Query Set - Tier 2 Government

**Persona:** Ministry official / policy analyst
**Access assertion:** Tier 2 responses must use institutional aggregates, anonymized summaries, and policy-level trends. Researcher PII must not be returned.

1. Which states had the highest AI publication growth from 2020 to 2024?
2. Compare total government innovation grant funding by institution type and year.
3. Which institutes convert grants into patents most efficiently, grouped by state?
4. Show the top research areas by national publication output and funding trend for the last five years.
5. Which ministries or agencies appear most often in funded research collaborations?
6. Compare IIT, NIT, and private-university research output per crore of funding.
7. Which states have strong renewable energy research but low patent commercialization?
8. Show aggregate TRL distribution by institute type for market-ready technologies.
9. Identify national collaboration clusters between institutions in AI, healthcare, and clean energy.
10. Generate a policy summary of underfunded high-output research areas without showing individual researcher details.

**Coverage:** institutional aggregates, state-level trends, funding efficiency, commercialization gaps, ministry collaboration patterns, and no researcher PII.
