# NRG Buyer Deck

## Slide 1: Problem

Indian research intelligence is fragmented across institutes, ministries, grants, publications, patents, labs, and collaborations. Decision makers cannot ask trusted cross-domain questions without manual reconciliation.

## Slide 2: Solution

National Research Graph is a sovereign research intelligence platform with natural-language querying, citation-backed answers, tiered access, DPDP-aware controls, and non-repudiable audit evidence.

## Slide 3: Market

Primary buyers are universities, IIT/NIT networks, ministries, state innovation missions, and R&D-heavy enterprises that need searchable research capability, funding visibility, collaboration mapping, and compliance-ready data access.

## Slide 4: Product

NRG combines PostgreSQL, Qdrant, Redis, Kong, Helm/Kubernetes deployment, schema-RAG, tier response filtering, red-team replay, vector drift monitoring, and evidence-backed handover workflows.

## Slide 5: Traction

The repo has launch-ready local evidence, production Helm charts, red-team tooling, UAT scripts, data-intake protocol, C4 load-test harness, and commercial handover artifacts.

## Slide 6: Team

Founder-led build with IITGN path, security-first architecture, and an operator-focused handover packet for institutional adoption.

## Slide 7: Ask

INR 50L to 1Cr for sovereign activation, official data ingest, external audit, first reference deployment, and 12 months of focused execution.

## Slide 8: Roadmap

30 days: entity, IP letter, cluster activation. 60 days: 600GB ingest, UAT, external audit start. 90 days: first reference deployment and buyer conversations.
